Tennessine (Ts, Z = 117): atomic energy levels

No energy level diagram can be drawn for neutral tennessine: no excited level of the atom has been measured, so there is no observed transition to show. This page lists what is known instead, and marks every calculated number as theory.

What has been measured

Nothing beyond nuclear decay properties: no atomic-physics measurement and no chemistry experiment (Yakushev et al. 2024 name Lv and Ts as targets of future chemical research).
