Roentgenium (Rg, Z = 111): atomic energy levels

No energy level diagram can be drawn for neutral roentgenium: no excited level of the atom has been measured, so there is no observed transition to show. This page lists what is known instead, and marks every calculated number as theory.

What has been measured

Nothing beyond nuclear decay properties: no atomic-physics measurement and no chemistry experiment (Steinegger 2021: Mt, Ds and Rg 'have yet dodged a chemical assessment completely').
