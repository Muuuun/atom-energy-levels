Mendelevium (Md, Z = 101): atomic energy levels

No energy level diagram can be drawn for neutral mendelevium: no excited level of the atom has been measured, so there is no observed transition to show. This page lists what is known instead, and marks every calculated number as theory.

What has been measured

Only the first ionisation potential, by surface ionisation of 251Md atoms one at a time on a hot tantalum surface followed by mass separation and alpha counting (6.59(13) eV). No atomic level or optical transition has been measured.
