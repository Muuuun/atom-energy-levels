Dubnium (Db, Z = 105): atomic energy levels

No energy level diagram can be drawn for neutral dubnium: no excited level of the atom has been measured, so there is no observed transition to show. This page lists what is known instead, and marks every calculated number as theory.

What has been measured

No atomic-physics measurement. Only chemistry with single atoms: liquid-phase studies of (oxo)halide complexes and gas-phase chromatography (the element was identified chemically by gas thermochromatography).
