All hyperfine constants are for 139La (I=7/2). They are copied from the machine-readable Tables 3 and 4 of Güzelçimen et al. 2022 (downloaded as text), which collect earlier measurements; each entry names the original reference given there and is marked "compilation" unless the constant was determined in the 2022 paper itself. In Table 3 (even levels) the error columns are printed in units of the last digit and were converted; in Table 4 (odd levels) they are in MHz. measured_energy_cm are the 2022 revised centre-of-gravity energies (global fit of Fourier-transform lines, hyperfine structure taken into account). Lifetimes: Den Hartog, Palmer, Lawler 2015, Table 1, read through an automated reader of the publisher page (two requests, 69 levels entered of the 72 reported); the 14 J=3/2 values were cross-checked against the independently parsed PDF table of Filin & Savukov 2020 and all agree. The paper quotes +-5 % for every lifetime. The names attached to earlier lifetimes in "alternatives" are those returned by the reader for the first half of the table; for levels above 24000 cm-1 the reference labels were not resolved. Transition frequencies are c times the centre-of-gravity vacuum wavenumbers of Table 2 of Güzelçimen et al. 2022 (natural La, i.e. 139La). A_s entries are the Den Hartog 2015 values (numerically the same as the NIST A values for 66 of 67 common lines), here with their uncertainties, taken second-hand from Filin & Savukov 2020 (J=3/2 upper levels only).
