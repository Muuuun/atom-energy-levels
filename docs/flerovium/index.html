Flerovium (Fl, Z = 114): atomic energy levels

No energy level diagram can be drawn for neutral flerovium: no excited level of the atom has been measured, so there is no observed transition to show. This page lists what is known instead, and marks every calculated number as theory.

What has been measured

No atomic-physics measurement. Only gas-phase adsorption chromatography of single atoms on gold and silicon oxide surfaces (first study: -dH_ads on gold = 34 (+54/-11) kJ/mol, quoted second-hand from Eichler et al. 2010); a volatile, weakly reactive metal.
