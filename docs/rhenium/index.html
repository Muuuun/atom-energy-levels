NIST ASD has no classified Re I lines; all transitions here are attached to NIST level energies and J. Three tiers of A_s: (1) Li et al. 2020, semi-empirical (calculated branching fraction x lifetime measured in that paper), method 'theory', 40 lines from 18 levels, plus 7 lines with the older semi-empirical values of Palmeri et al. 2006 quoted in the same table; (2) Duquette, Salih & Lawler 1982 (measured lifetime x branching ratio) taken second-hand from the Kurucz line list gf7500.all (reference code DSL), method 'compilation', 46 lines including the strong 346.0 / 346.5 / 345.2 nm resonance lines and the 488.9 nm intercombination line to z 8P7/2; (3) Corliss & Bozman 1962 arc-intensity values from the same Kurucz list (code CB) for the remaining lines from the ground state and for lines to levels below 30000 cm-1, method 'compilation', low accuracy. Kurucz log gf were converted with A = 0.66702 sigma^2 gf / (2J'+1), sigma in cm-1 from the level energies (derived). The Kurucz list contains about 680 more Corliss-Bozman Re I lines between excited levels that were not entered. Wavelengths in labels are air wavelengths computed from level energies. Isotope abundances in percent; abundance_unc in percent.
