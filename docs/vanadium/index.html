Lifetimes: where several LIF measurements exist the entry is the one with the smallest relative uncertainty; the others are under lifetime.alternatives. Values labelled Den Hartog 2014, Whaling 1985, Doerr 1985, Rudolph & Helbig 1982, Xu 2006 are second-hand (read in the comparison tables of Holmes et al. 2016 and Wang et al. 2014, VizieR copies). Transition probabilities are Lawler et al. 2014 Table 3 (VizieR), for every line whose upper level is drawn on the site plus the z4I°15/2 cooling level; NIST ASD V I A-values (its reference T9399) appear to be the same data set, so these mainly add the printed uncertainties. Holmes et al. 2016 report that Lawler et al. underestimate log(gf) near 9000+-100 A and suggest a correction of +0.18(3) dex there. Hyperfine A are for 51V (I = 7/2), printed in mK, MHz derived by unit conversion. rme_J_ea0 values are derived from A_s. Level energies in the sources (Thorne et al. 2011) coincide with the NIST values. "drawn_on_site" marks what data/v/levels.csv / transitions.csv contain.
