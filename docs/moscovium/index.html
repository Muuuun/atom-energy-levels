Moscovium (Mc, Z = 115): atomic energy levels

No energy level diagram can be drawn for neutral moscovium: no excited level of the atom has been measured, so there is no observed transition to show. This page lists what is known instead, and marks every calculated number as theory.

What has been measured

No atomic-physics measurement. Only gas-solid chromatography of single 288Mc atoms: adsorption enthalpy on silicon oxide -dH_ads = 54 (+11/-5) kJ/mol, a chemical (not spectroscopic) quantity.
