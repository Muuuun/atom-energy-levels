Gadolinium energy level diagram

Energy levels and transitions of neutral gadolinium (Gd I): the 19 strongest classified lines below 2 µm from the NIST Atomic Spectra Database and the 21 levels they connect, each line with its vacuum wavelength, frequency and, for 17 of them, the reduced dipole matrix element derived from the NIST transition rate. Measured lifetimes, transition rates, hyperfine constants and isotope shifts from the literature are included, each with its citation.

Stable isotopes: 155Gd and 157Gd have I = 3/2 (both nuclear moments negative: mu = -0.2591 and -0.3398 nuclear magnetons in the IAEA table); the others have I = 0. Hyperfine convention: E = A K/2 + B [3K(K+1) - 4I(I+1)J(J+1)] / [8I(2I-1)J(2J-1)], K = F(F+1)-J(J+1)-I(I+1) (as printed by Adhikari et al.). The LLNL constants (ground level 9D°2, 17380.827 level, 575.19 nm line) are modeling inputs printed without signs or uncertainties in a table that exists only as an image (read from the rendered PDF page); treat them as indicative. Isotope-shift keys "A-B" mean nu(A) - nu(B). This file is thin because the two key Wisconsin papers (lifetimes and transition probabilities, 2011) could not be opened.
