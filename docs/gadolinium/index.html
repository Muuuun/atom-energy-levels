Gadolinium energy level diagram

Energy levels and transitions of neutral gadolinium (Gd I): the 60 strongest classified lines below 2 µm from the NIST Atomic Spectra Database and the 61 levels they connect, each line with its vacuum wavelength, frequency and, for 22 of them, the reduced dipole matrix element derived from the NIST transition rate. Measured lifetimes, transition rates, hyperfine constants and isotope shifts from the literature are included, each with its citation. 12 further lines lead from the ground level to a long-lived level (the NIST level list has no lower level that an electric-dipole decay could reach). The NIST line list does not carry them: the wavelength follows from the two level energies, the type is the lowest multipole that J and parity allow (M1, E2, M2, or J = 0 – 0), and no rate is given.

Gadolinium lines in brief

  • Gd 9D°2 → 9P3 (proposed laser-cooling line (368 nm, 9D°2 - 9P3); not closed: the upper level also decays to 9D°3 (371 nm) and 9D°4, so a repumper at 371 nm is needed (Battles thesis 2020; proposal, no experiment)): 368.5183 nm in vacuum, 368.4134 nm in air, 813.5077 THz; Einstein A = 9.1 × 107 s⁻¹ (NIST); lifetime of the upper level 3.5 ns (measured).
  • Gd 9D°3 → 9P3 (371 nm repumper for the proposed 368 nm cooling scheme (Battles thesis 2020)): 371.4631 nm in vacuum, 371.3575 nm in air, 807.0584 THz; Einstein A = 9.2 × 107 s⁻¹ (NIST); lifetime of the upper level 3.5 ns (measured).
  • Gd 9D°6 → (25376)7 (proposed laser-cooling line (423 nm, J = 6 -> 7, treated as closed two-level system for the bosonic isotopes; Battles thesis 2020; proposal, no experiment); also the reference line of King plots (422 nm)): 422.7038 nm in vacuum, 422.5848 nm in air, 709.2258 THz; Einstein A = 8.9 × 107 s⁻¹ (NIST); lifetime of the upper level 9.4 ns (measured).
  • Gd 9D°2 → 9D3 (443.06 nm (air) line addressed in a Zeeman-slower experiment with a broadband 447.1 nm diode laser (Chavarría-Sibaja et al. 2021)): 443.1873 nm in vacuum, 443.0629 nm in air, 676.4464 THz; Einstein A = 3 × 107 s⁻¹ (NIST); lifetime of the upper level 13.7 ns (measured).
  • Gd 9D°3 → (22335)2 (451.96 nm (air) line addressed in a Zeeman-slower experiment with a broadband 447.1 nm diode laser (Chavarría-Sibaja et al. 2021)): 452.0922 nm in vacuum, 451.9654 nm in air, 663.1224 THz; Einstein A = 3.8 × 107 s⁻¹ (NIST); lifetime of the upper level 10.8 ns (measured).
  • Gd 9D°2 → 9F2 (first step (575.19 nm) of the LLNL 575.190 -> 581.037 -> 623.347 nm three-step photoionization scheme for gadolinium laser isotope separation): 575.3466 nm in vacuum, 575.1871 nm in air, 521.0641 THz; Einstein A = 6.86 × 105 s⁻¹ (measured); lifetime of the upper level 700 ns (measured).
  • Gd 9D°4 → 11F3 (726.266 nm spin-forbidden line 9D°4 - 11F3 studied as a candidate for narrow-line laser cooling / optical clock work (Adhikari et al. 2019)): 726.4626 nm in vacuum, 726.2624 nm in air, 412.6743 THz.
  • Gd 9D°6 → 11F5 (743.014 nm spin-forbidden line 9D°6 - 11F5 studied as a candidate for narrow-line laser cooling / optical clock work; King-plot nonlinearity reported (Adhikari et al. 2019)): 743.2229 nm in vacuum, 743.0183 nm in air, 403.3681 THz.

Wavelengths and frequencies from the NIST level energies unless a measured frequency is listed below; every number is repeated with its source in the tables of this page.

Hyperfine convention: E = A K/2 + B [3K(K+1) - 4I(I+1)J(J+1)] / [8I(2I-1)J(2J-1)], K = F(F+1)-J(J+1)-I(I+1) (as printed by Adhikari et al.).
