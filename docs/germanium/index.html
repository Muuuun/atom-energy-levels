Germanium energy level diagram

Energy levels and transitions of neutral germanium (Ge I): the 54 strongest classified lines below 2 µm from the NIST Atomic Spectra Database and the 20 levels they connect, each line with its vacuum wavelength, frequency and, for 48 of them, the reduced dipole matrix element derived from the NIST transition rate. Measured lifetimes, transition rates, hyperfine constants and isotope shifts from the literature are included, each with its citation.

Level energies are the NIST ASD values (data/nist/Ge_I_levels.tsv); Li et al. and Biemont et al. quote the same levels rounded to 0.01 cm^-1. A_s of E1 lines = measured branching ratio x 1/(measured laser lifetime) as printed by the authors; rme_J_ea0 is derived from that A with the NIST energies (convention of SCHEMA.md, J' = upper level). 4p5s lifetimes and 4p2-4p5s A values come from Biemont et al. 1999 (read directly; Li et al. 1999 reprints them), 4p4d / 4p6s / 4p5d lifetimes and 4p2-4p4d A values from Li et al. 1999. Forbidden-line rates and the metastable lifetimes derived from them are 1964 intermediate-coupling THEORY (Garstang), not measurements. Isotope shifts are nu(first) - nu(second), centre of gravity, natural reference isotope 72Ge. Hyperfine constants refer to 73Ge (I = 9/2). Deverall, Meissner, Zissis, Phys. Rev. 95, 1463 (1954) (abstract) report no resolvable structure in eight Ge I lines and estimate the shift between consecutive even isotopes as smaller than 0.005 cm^-1 (bound).
