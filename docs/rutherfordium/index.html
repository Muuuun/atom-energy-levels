Rutherfordium (Rf, Z = 104): atomic energy levels

No energy level diagram can be drawn for neutral rutherfordium: no excited level of the atom has been measured, so there is no observed transition to show. This page lists what is known instead, and marks every calculated number as theory.

What has been measured

No atomic-physics measurement. Only chemistry with single atoms: liquid-phase studies of (oxo)halide complexes and gas-phase adsorption chromatography of volatile compounds.
