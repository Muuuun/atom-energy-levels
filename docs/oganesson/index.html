Oganesson (Og, Z = 118): atomic energy levels

No energy level diagram can be drawn for neutral oganesson: no excited level of the atom has been measured, so there is no observed transition to show. This page lists what is known instead, and marks every calculated number as theory.

What has been measured

Nothing beyond nuclear decay properties: no atomic-physics measurement and no chemistry experiment (production rate about one atom per month).
