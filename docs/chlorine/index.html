Chlorine energy level diagram

Energy levels and transitions of neutral chlorine (Cl I): the 79 strongest classified lines below 2 µm from the NIST Atomic Spectra Database and the 43 levels they connect, each line with its vacuum wavelength, frequency and, for 75 of them, the reduced dipole matrix element derived from the NIST transition rate. Measured lifetimes, transition rates, hyperfine constants and isotope shifts from the literature are included, each with its citation.
