Lawrencium (Lr, Z = 103): atomic energy levels

No energy level diagram can be drawn for neutral lawrencium: no excited level of the atom has been measured, so there is no observed transition to show. This page lists what is known instead, and marks every calculated number as theory.

What has been measured

The first ionisation potential, by surface ionisation of 256Lr atoms one at a time (4.96 +0.05/-0.04 eV), and a lower limit of 13.0 eV for the second ionisation potential from a charge-exchange reaction (second-hand, Kwarsick et al., cited by Rickert et al.). A laser search at GSI (two-step resonance ionisation of 255Lr, about 1500 cm-1 scanned in total around the transitions predicted near 20 300 and 28 600 cm-1) found no atomic transition.
