Calcium-40 energy level diagram

Energy levels and transitions of neutral 40Ca (Ca I, nuclear spin I = 0): 35 levels and 61 lines below 2 µm, each with its vacuum wavelength, frequency and, for 57 of them, the reduced dipole matrix element. Level energies come from the NIST Atomic Spectra Database; transition rates are 53 NIST, 5 measured. Neutral calcium is also the starting point for loading Ca+ ion traps by photoionisation.

Isotope shifts are nu(A) - nu(40), in MHz; for 43Ca they refer to the hyperfine centre of gravity. Frequencies refer to 40Ca. Hyperfine A, B follow the usual convention (43Ca has a negative nuclear moment, mu = -1.317643 mu_N, I = 7/2). Extra (non-schema) fields used: linewidth_Hz / linewidth_kHz / linewidth_MHz (natural linewidth, FWHM, as printed), wavelength_vac_nm, zeeman, note, alternatives (other measurements of the same quantity), top-level ionization and photoionization blocks. Lifetimes marked 'compilation' are the experimental values collected in Table 1 of Den Hartog et al. 2021 (laser-based methods only), which was read from a rendered image of the arXiv PDF because its text layer drops digits. The NIST Ca I levels file has no Lande-g column; the two measured g_J values given here are the only ones found. The 1D2 -> 3P1,2 rates (300 and 96 s^-1) and the 1D2 total lifetime are poorly known: experiment 1.5(4) ms versus theory 1.71 ms, while 300 + 96 + 40 s^-1 would give 2.3 ms.
