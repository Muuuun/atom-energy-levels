Energy levels and transitions of neutral 9Be (Be I, nuclear spin I = 3/2): 17 levels and 26 lines below 2 µm, each with its vacuum wavelength, frequency and, for 22 of them, the reduced dipole matrix element. Level energies come from the NIST Atomic Spectra Database; transition rates are 26 NIST. Neutral beryllium is also the starting point for loading Be+ ion traps by photoionisation.

A transition is closed when its upper level can decay only to the level it was excited from, so the atom keeps scattering photons. “closed”: among the levels NIST lists, no other level below the upper level has the opposite parity and |ΔJ| ≤ 1, so no other electric-dipole decay exists. Otherwise the leak is the sum of the branching ratios of the other decay lines of the upper level (or 1 − branching ratio where that of the line itself has been measured), and the number of photons is 1 / leak. “≥” and “≤”: at least one possible decay path has no listed rate, so the leak is a lower limit. These numbers are derived from the branching ratios, lifetimes and rates of this page, not measured as such; the tag names the weakest of the inputs, * marks theory and ≈ a model calculation. Listed: lines that start on the ground level or on a metastable level and leak at most 10 %, unless more than three possible decay paths have no listed rate. Fine-structure levels only: hyperfine and Zeeman dark states and forbidden decays without a listed rate are not considered. Select a line in the diagram for the full list of its leak channels.

All energies identifying levels are copied from data/nist/Be_I_levels.tsv. 9Be is the only stable isotope (I = 3/2). Hyperfine sign convention: A and B of 2s2p 3P1,2 are the Blachman-Lurio constants AFTER second/third-order corrections for hyperfine mixing among the 3P_J levels (A negative because the 9Be nuclear moment is negative). First-order constants fitted directly to the same intervals (A1 = -140.1564, B1 = -3.2363, A2 = -124.6165, B2 = 0.7800 MHz; Puchalski et al. 2021) are a different definition - do not mix. The abstract of arXiv:2411.13013 quotes the old B(3P2) as 1.427(9) MHz while its Table VI and arXiv:2603.11098 give 1.429(8) MHz; the table value is used. Fine structure of 2s2p 3P: Blachman & Lurio (abstract) deduced W(3P2)-W(3P1) = 71.86(24) GHz and W(3P1)-W(3P0) = 19.41(19) GHz from the magnetic-field dependence of the hyperfine structure (not a direct measurement); Puchalski et al. 2021 calculate 71.379 and 19.498 GHz and suspect the experimental 3P2-3P1 value is inaccurate; the NIST level differences are 2.335 and 0.615 cm^-1. Lifetimes taken from Table IV of arXiv:2603.11098 are marked 'compilation' with the original paper named; for multiplets the measurement is a term value and is repeated on each J level. Where several measurements exist the laser (cascade-free) result is the main value and beam-foil results are in 'alternatives'; for 2p2 3P, 2s3d 1D and 2s4d 1D the main value is Bromander 1971 (a re-analysis of the Bergstrom 1969 Stockholm decay curves
