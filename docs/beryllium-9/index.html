Beryllium-9 energy level diagram

Energy levels and transitions of neutral 9Be (Be I, nuclear spin I = 3/2): 15 levels and 24 lines below 2 µm, each with its vacuum wavelength, frequency and, for 20 of them, the reduced dipole matrix element. Level energies come from the NIST Atomic Spectra Database; transition rates are 24 NIST. Neutral beryllium is also the starting point for loading Be+ ion traps by photoionisation.
